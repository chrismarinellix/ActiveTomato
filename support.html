ActiveTomato — Support

A calm, distraction-free Pomodoro focus timer.

Getting started

  • Tap Sign in with Apple once — no password needed.
  • Press Start to begin a 25-minute focus session.
  • Use the Focus / Short / Long tabs to switch modes.
  • Use Chain to link several sessions into a deep-work run.

Your progress

You earn points for each focus session and level up from Seedling to Forest. The activity grid shows your focus history, and everything syncs privately across your Apple devices via iCloud.

Settings

In Settings you can turn on/off sound cues, progress beeps, the countdown tick, voice cues, and focus reminders, and adjust the volume.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account to erase all of your data from your device and iCloud.
