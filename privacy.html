ActiveTomato — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 June 2026

ActiveTomato is a Pomodoro focus timer. It is built to respect your privacy: the developer does not run any server, does not collect your data, and does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What data the app handles

ActiveTomato stores your focus sessions, points, streak history, and settings. This information is kept on your device and in your own private iCloud account (via Apple's iCloud/CloudKit) so it can sync across your Apple devices. The developer cannot see or access this data — it lives in your personal iCloud, controlled by you.

Sign in with Apple

ActiveTomato uses Sign in with Apple to sign you in. You may choose to hide your email address, and the app never sells, shares, or transmits your name or email to the developer or any third party. The sign-in identifier is used only to associate the app with you on your devices.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Deleting your data

You can erase all of your data at any time from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your points, history, and synced data from your device and your iCloud.

Children

ActiveTomato is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
